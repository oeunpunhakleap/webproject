Somlor Tropeang

សម្លត្រពាំង

Somlor Tropeang is a traditional Cambodian soup made with baby bamboo shoots, fish, coconut milk, and prahok. It has a rich, slightly creamy taste with a unique aroma from fermented fish and herbs.

Duration 45m
Calories 850 kcal (280 kcal per serving)

  1. Step 1: Prepare bamboo

    • Boil bamboo shoots to remove bitterness
    • Drain and set aside

  2. Step 2: Cook rice base

    • Boil water in a pot
    • Add rice and cook until soft

  3. Step 3: Add spices

    • Add lemongrass, galangal, garlic, and prahok
    • Stir well

  4. Step 4: Add coconut milk

    • Pour coconut milk into the pot
    • Mix gently

  5. Step 5: Add bamboo

    • Add bamboo shoots
    • Cook until tender

  6. Step 6: Add fish

    • Add fish pieces
    • Add luffa
    • Cook for 5–7 minutes

  7. Step 7: Season

    • Add sugar and fish sauce
    • Taste and adjust

  8. Step 8: Finish

    • Add Slerk Bas, Bonlea P'em and Chili leaves or chili (optional)
    • Serve hot with rice
