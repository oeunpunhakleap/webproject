Braised Pork with Eggs

ខសាច់ជ្រូកពង

This dish is made by slowly cooking pork belly and boiled eggs in a caramel sauce using sugar, fish sauce, and coconut water. It has a rich, sweet and savory flavor with tender meat and a dark glossy sauce.

  1. Step1: Prepare the eggs

    • Boil eggs until fully cooked
    • Peel and set aside

  2. Step 2: Make caramel

    • Heat sugar in a pot
    • Cook until golden brown

  3. Step 3: Cook pork

    • Add pork belly and garlic
    • Stir until slightly browned

  4. Step 4: Add seasoning

    • Add fish sauce and pepper
    • Mix well

  5. Step 5: Add liquid

    • Pour coconut water
    • Bring to a boil

  6. Step 6: Simmer

    • Add boiled eggs
    • Cook for 30–40 minutes
    • Let sauce reduce and thicken

  7. Step 7: Serve

    • Serve hot with rice
