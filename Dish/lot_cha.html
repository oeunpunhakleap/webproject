Lot Cha

ឡូតឆា

Lot Cha is a popular Cambodian street food made with short rice noodles stir-fried with vegetables, egg, and savory sauce. It has a smoky flavor and is often served with fresh herbs and lime.

Duration 15m

  1. Step 1: Prepare ingredients

    • Wash bean sprouts, cabbage and chives
    • Chop garlic and cut vegetables into bite-sized pieces
    • Set everything ready

  2. Step 2: Cook pork

    • Heat oil in a pan
    • Add garlic and stir until fragrant
    • Add beef and cook until done

  3. Step 3: Add noodles

    • Add Lot Cha noodles
    • Stir fry on high heat

  4. Step 4: Season

    • Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce and sugar
    • Mix well

  5. Step 5: Add vegetables

    • Add bean sprouts, cabbage and chives
    • Stir quickly

  6. Step 6: Add egg

    • Push noodles to one side
    • Crack eggs and scramble
    • Mix everything together

  7. Step 7: Finish

    • Add egg on the top
    • Serve hot with chili sauce
