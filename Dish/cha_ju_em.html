Sweet and Sour Pork Ribs

ឆាឆ្អឹងជំនីជូអែម

This Cambodian dish features crispy pork ribs stir-fried in a flavorful sweet and sour sauce made with garlic, ketchup, fish sauce, and sugar. It is balanced, tangy, and delicious.

  1. Step 1: Prepare ribs

    • Cut pork ribs into small pieces
    • Wash and drain well

  2. Step 2: Marinate

    • Mix ribs with soy sauce, fish sauce, oyster sauce, pepper, and a little sugar
    • Add eggs, crispy fried powder and mix well
    • Marinate for 15–20 minutes

  3. Step 3: Fry ribs

    • Heat oil in a pan
    • Fry ribs until golden brown
    • Remove and set aside

  4. Step 4: Make sauce

    • Add garlic and cook until fragrant
    • Add chili sauce, fish sauce, sugar, oyster sauce and a little water
    • Stir until slightly thick

  5. Step 5: Add vegetables

    • Add onion, greenbell pepper, tomato and pineapple
    • Stir fry briefly

  6. Step 6: Combine

    • Add ribs back into the pan
    • Mix well with sauce
    • Cook 2–3 minutes

  7. Step 7: Serve

    • Serve hot with rice
