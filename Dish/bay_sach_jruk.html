Bay Sach Jruk

បាយសាច់ជ្រូក

Bay Sach Jruk is one of Cambodia’s most popular breakfast dishes, made with thinly sliced pork marinated in garlic, soy sauce, fish sauce, and coconut milk, then grilled until tender and slightly caramelized. It is served with rice, fresh vegetables, pickles, and often a light soup, offering a perfect balance of sweet, savory, and smoky flavors.

Recipe

  1. Step 1: Marinate the pork

    • Mix garlic, soy sauce, fish sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar
    • Add sliced pork and mix well
    • Marinate for at least 1 hour (or overnight for best flavor)

  2. Step 2: Grill the pork

    • Heat a grill or pan (charcoal grill is best)
    • Cook pork until golden brown and slightly charred
    • Turn occasionally to get even color

  3. Step 3: Prepare vegetables

    • Slice cucumber, radish, and carrot
    • Mix all vegetables together with lemons
    • Add salt , sugar, lemons, garlic and fish sauce to taste

  4. Step 4: Prepare oil sauce

    • Heat oil in a pan
    • Add garlic and sauté(optional) until fragrant
    • Add soy sauce, fish sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar
    • Add green onion
    • Stir well and let it simmer for a few minutes

  5. Step 5: Serve

    • Place rice on a plate
    • Add grilled pork on top
    • Serve with vegetables and and oil sauce
    • Add fried egg on top (optional)
    • Optional: serve with a bowl of light soup would be more delicious
