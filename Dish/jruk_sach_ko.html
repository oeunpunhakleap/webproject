Beef Soup with Lemongrass & Lime

ស្ងោរសាច់គោ

This Cambodian soup is light, aromatic, and refreshing. It uses lemongrass, garlic, and lime to create a sour and savory broth, combined with tender beef and fresh herbs.

  1. Step 1: Prepare ingredients

    • Slice beef thinly
    • Chop spring onion and herbs
    • Crush garlic, lemongrass, and galangal
    • Cut lime and chili

  2. Step 2: Boil water with spices

    • Bring water to a boil
    • Add lemongrass, garlic, and galangal
    • Let it boil to release aroma

  3. Step 3: Add beef

    • Add sliced beef into boiling water
    • Cook until just done

  4. Step 4: Season

    • Add fish sauce and sugar
    • Add chili if desired
    • Squeeze lime juice

  5. Step 5: Finish

    • Add spring onion
    • Turn off heat

  6. Step 6: Serve

    • Serve hot with rice
