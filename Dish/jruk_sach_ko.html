Sngor Sach Ko

ស្ងោរសាច់គោ

Sngor Sach Ko is a traditional Cambodian beef stew made by slow-cooking beef with lemongrass, galangal, and other aromatic spices. It has a rich, savory flavor and tender meat that falls apart easily.

Duration 15m
Calories 600 kcal (200 kcal per serving)
Rating 4.8/5

  1. Step 1: Prepare ingredients

    • Slice beef thinly
    • Chop spring onion and herbs
    • Crush garlic, lemongrass, and galangal
    • Cut lime and chili

  2. Step 2: Boil water with spices

    • Bring water to a boil
    • Add lemongrass, garlic, and galangal
    • Let it boil to release aroma

  3. Step 3: Add beef

    • Add sliced beef into boiling water
    • Cook until just done

  4. Step 4: Season

    • Add fish sauce and sugar
    • Add chili if desired
    • Squeeze lime juice

  5. Step 5: Finish

    • Add spring onion
    • Turn off heat

  6. Step 6: Serve

    • Serve hot with rice
