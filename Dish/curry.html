Chicken Curry

ការីមាន់

Khmer Chicken Curry is a rich, aromatic Cambodian dish made with chicken, coconut milk, and a fragrant curry paste. It has a slightly sweet, savory flavor and is typically served with bread or rice noodle.

Recipe

  1. Step 1: Prepare the chicken and vegetables

    • Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces
    • Peel and chop potatoes and carrots into chunks
    • Set aside

  2. Step 2: Make the curry paste

    • Blend or pound lemongrass, galangal, turmeric, garlic, shallots, and kaffir lime leaves
    • Process until smooth

  3. Step 3: Cook the curry base

    • Heat a little oil in a pan
    • Add the curry paste and fry until fragrant
    • Add coconut milk and mix well

  4. Step 4: Add chicken and vegetables

    • Add chicken pieces to the curry base
    • Add potatoes and carrots
    • Simmer until chicken is cooked and vegetables are tender

  5. Step 5: Season the curry

    • Add fish sauce and sugar to taste
    • Adjust seasoning as needed

  6. Step 6: Garnish and serve

    • Top with sliced red chili (optional)
    • Serve hot with steamed rice
