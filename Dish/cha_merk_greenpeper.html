Delicious Squid with Green Peppercorn

ឆាមឹកម្រេចខ្ចី

This Cambodian dish features fresh squid stir-fried with green peppercorn, garlic, and savory sauces. It is aromatic, slightly spicy, and highlights the natural sweetness of seafood.

  1. Step 1: Prepare squid

    • Clean the squid and remove the inside
    • Cut into rings or bite-sized pieces
    • Set aside

  2. Step 2: Prepare ingredients

    • Chop garlic finely
    • Slice onion and green bell pepper
    • Cut chili if using

  3. Step 3: Stir fry garlic

    • Heat cooking oil in a pan
    • Add garlic and cook until fragrant

  4. Step 4: Cook squid

    • Add squid and stir fry quickly (1–2 minutes)
    • Avoid overcooking

  5. Step 5: Add vegetables

    • Add onion, green bell pepper, chili, and green peppercorn
    • Stir fry briefly

  6. Step 6: Season

    • Add fish sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar
    • Mix well

  7. Step 7: Finish

    • Taste and adjust seasoning
    • Serve immediately while hot
