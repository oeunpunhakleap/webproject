Cha Kdav Sach Morn

ឆាក្ដៅសាច់មាន់

Cha Kdav Sach Morn is a Cambodian stir-fried chicken dish cooked over high heat with garlic, chili, and savory sauces.

  1. Step 1: Prepare ingredients

    • Cut chicken into small pieces
    • Chop garlic, lemon grass, galangal, and turmeric
    • Then mixed/pounded it together

  2. Step 2: Cook garlic

    • Heat oil in a pan
    • Add garlic and the pounded of lemon grass, galangal, turmeric, and stir until fragrant

  3. Step 3: Cook chicken

    • Add chicken
    • Stir-fry until cooked

  4. Step 4: Add some chili

    • Add chili
    • Stir well

  5. Step 5: Add seasoning

    • Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar, and pepper
    • Mix well

  6. Step 6: Finish

    • Add holy basil
    • Cook 1–2 more minutes
    • Serve hot with rice
