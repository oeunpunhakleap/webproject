Minced Pork and Napa Cabbage Soup

ស៊ុបស្ពៃបូកគោ

Minced Pork and Napa Cabbage Soup is a comforting and flavorful Cambodian dish made with ground pork, napa cabbage, and a savory broth. It is often enjoyed as a light meal or appetizer, perfect for warming up on a chilly day.

Duration 30m
Calorie 350 kcal per serving
Rating 4.5/5

Ingredients

Ground Pork - 200g

Napa Cabbage - 1/2 head

Garlic - 3 cloves

Ginger - 1 inch piece

Chicken Broth - 4 cups

Fish Sauce - 2 tbsp

White Pepper - 1/2 tsp

Green Onions - 2 stalks

  1. Step 1: Prepare ingredients

    • Wash and cut napa cabbage into pieces
    • Slice carrot and radish into small pieces
    • Cut shiitake mushrooms
    • Chop garlic, shallots, and green onions

  2. Step 2: Stir-fry base

    • Heat cooking oil in a pot
    • Add garlic, dried shrimp, dried stingray and stir until fragrant
    • Add shiitake mushrooms and stir-fry first

  3. Step 3: Add water

    • Pour in water
    • Bring to a boil

  4. Step 4: Add minced pork

    • Add minced pork into the boiling soup
    • Stir gently to separate the meat
    • Cook until pork is fully done

  5. Step 5: Add vegetables

    • Add napa cabbage, carrot, and radish
    • Cook until vegetables are tender

  6. Step 6: Finish and serve

    • Add fish sauce and black pepper to taste
    • Add green onions
    • Serve hot with rice
