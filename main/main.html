About Us

Jeat Nas was created in 2026 with a vision to help you think less about what to cook and enjoy the process of discovering Khmer cuisine.

Developed by a team of students from the American University of Phnom Penh, this platform combines technology and culture to make traditional recipes more accessible and engaging.

Based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, Jeat Nas aims to preserve and promote Khmer culinary heritage through a modern, user-friendly experience.

A high-end, user-friendly recipe hub focused on the "how-to" of traditional Khmer cooking, featuring curated recipes, cultural storytelling, and an interactive ingredient system.
